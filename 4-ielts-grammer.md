# ধাপ ১: Basic

## 1. Tenses

IELTS-এ সবচেয়ে বেশি লাগে এই ৫টা tense। এগুলো ঠিক থাকলে অর্ধেক ভুল কমে যাবে।

### 1.1 Present Simple: অভ্যাস, সাধারণ সত্য
**গঠন:** Subject + V1 (he/she/it হলে V1 + s/es)

- I **wake up** at 7 am every day.
- She **works** in a software company.
- Water **boils** at 100°C.

**Signal words:** usually, always, often, every day, generally

### 1.2 Past Simple: অতীতে শেষ হয়ে যাওয়া কাজ
**গঠন:** Subject + V2

- I **woke up** late this morning.
- I **graduated** in Physics in 2013.
- I **started** my career **in** 2014.

**Signal words:** yesterday, last year, ago, in 2014, this morning (যদি সকাল শেষ হয়ে গিয়ে থাকে)

⚠️ **তোমার ভুল:** অতীতের ঘটনায় present tense লেখা।
- ❌ Yesterday I **go** to office.
- ✅ Yesterday I **went** to the office.

### 1.3 Present Continuous: এখন চলছে / সাময়িক
**গঠন:** am/is/are + V-ing

- I **am preparing** for IELTS these days.
- She **is learning** React right now.

### 1.4 Present Perfect: অতীতে শুরু, ফল এখনো আছে / অভিজ্ঞতা
**গঠন:** have/has + V3

- I **have worked** on more than 50 projects.
- I **have** never **been** to Europe.
- I **have** just **finished** my breakfast.

**Signal words:** ever, never, already, yet, just, so far

### 1.5 Present Perfect Continuous: অতীতে শুরু, এখনো চলছে
**গঠন:** have/has been + V-ing + since/for

- I **have been working** as a web developer **since** 2014.
- I **have been learning** English **for** two years.

⚠️ **তোমার ভুল:**
- ❌ I **started** working **since** 2014 **to till now**.
- ✅ I **started** working **in** 2014. (শুধু শুরুর কথা)
- ✅ I **have been working since** 2014. (শুরু হয়েছে, এখনো চলছে)

**since vs for:**
| since + নির্দিষ্ট সময় | for + সময়ের পরিমাণ |
|---|---|
| since 2014 | for 10 years |
| since Monday | for three months |
| since I was a child | for a long time |

### 1.6 Future: will / be going to
- I **will** probably **apply** to German universities. (সিদ্ধান্ত/অনুমান)
- I **am going to take** the IELTS exam in March. (আগে থেকে ঠিক করা plan)

### Tense consistency নিয়ম
একটা গল্প অতীতে শুরু করলে পুরোটা অতীতেই রাখো। মাঝখানে হঠাৎ present-এ চলে যেও না।
- ❌ Last year I **joined** a company and I **work** there for six months.
- ✅ Last year I **joined** a company and **worked** there for six months.

## 2. Articles (a / an / the)

| Article | কখন | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | একটা, অনির্দিষ্ট, consonant **sound**-এর আগে | a developer, a university, a job |
| **an** | একটা, অনির্দিষ্ট, vowel **sound**-এর আগে | an engineer, an hour, an MS degree |
| **the** | নির্দিষ্ট, যেটা দুজনেই চিনি / একটাই আছে | the job market, the internet, the best option |
| **(কিছু না)** | general plural / uncountable / দেশের নাম | Developers earn well. Software is useful. I live in Bangladesh. |

**মনে রাখো:**
- পেশা বলার সময় **a/an** লাগবেই: I work **as a** developer. She is **an** engineer.
- **sound** দেখে ঠিক করো, অক্ষর দেখে না: **a** university (ইউ), **an** hour (আওয়ার), **an** MS (এম)
- Superlative-এর আগে **the**: **the** most important, **the** best
- একটাই আছে এমন জিনিস: **the** sun, **the** internet, **the** world
- কিছু দেশে the লাগে: **the** UK, **the** USA, **the** Netherlands

⚠️ **তোমার ভুল:**
- ❌ I work as developer. → ✅ I work as **a** developer.
- ❌ Job market is very competitive. → ✅ **The** job market is very competitive.

---

## 3. Subject–Verb Agreement

**নিয়ম:** Subject singular হলে verb singular, plural হলে verb plural।

| Subject | Verb |
|---|---|
| He / She / It / একজন মানুষ | works, is, has, does |
| I / You / We / They / অনেকজন | work, are, have, do |

- My brother **works** in Dhaka.
- My parents **live** in Cumilla.

**কঠিন কেসগুলো:**
- **Everyone / Everybody / Each / Nobody** → singular: Everyone **wants** a good job.
- **People** → plural: People **are** busy nowadays.
- **One of the + plural noun** → verb singular: One of my friends **lives** in Germany.
- **The number of** → singular: The number of students **is** increasing.
- **A number of** → plural: A number of students **are** going abroad.

---

## 4. Singular / Plural

- **one of the + plural noun**
  - ❌ one of the best **candidate** → ✅ one of the best **candidates**
  - ✅ one of the biggest **problems**
- **many / several / a few / these / those** → plural noun
  - many **countries**, several **projects**, a few **years**
- **Uncountable noun** (s হয় না, a/an বসে না):
  - information, advice, knowledge, experience (অভিজ্ঞতা অর্থে), software, equipment, research, homework, traffic
  - ❌ informations, advices, softwares → ✅ some information, a piece of advice
  - "experience" বলতে একটা ঘটনা বোঝালে countable: I had **an** interesting **experience**.

---

## 5. Prepositions

### সময়
| in | on | at |
|---|---|---|
| মাস, বছর, ঋতু, সকাল/বিকাল | দিন, তারিখ | নির্দিষ্ট সময় |
| in 2014, in March, in the morning | on Monday, on 5 May | at 7 am, at night, at the weekend (UK) |

### জায়গা
- **in**: শহর, দেশ, বন্ধ জায়গা → in Dhaka, in a company, in the room
- **at**: নির্দিষ্ট point → at home, at work, at the university
- **on**: surface / platform → on the website, on GitHub, on the second floor

### খুব কমন verb/adjective + preposition
| ✅ সঠিক | ❌ ভুল |
|---|---|
| interested **in** | interested about |
| good **at** | good in |
| depend **on** | depend upon/to |
| married **to** | married with |
| responsible **for** | responsible of |
| apply **for** a job / **to** a university | apply in |
| graduate **from** a university | graduate in a university |
| listen **to** music | listen music |
| discuss something (no preposition) | discuss about |

# ধাপ ২: Band 6 → 6.5+

IELTS-এ "Grammatical Range" নম্বর পেতে শুধু simple sentence দিলে হবে না। জটিল sentence-ও লাগবে, এবং সেগুলো ঠিক হতে হবে।

## 6. Complex Sentences

### 6.1 Relative clause (who / which / that / where)
- **who** → মানুষ: I have a friend **who** lives in Berlin.
- **which** → জিনিস: I use Next.js, **which** is a React framework.
- **that** → মানুষ/জিনিস দুটোই (comma ছাড়া): This is the project **that** I built last year.
- **where** → জায়গা: Dhaka is the city **where** I grew up.

### 6.2 Subordinate clause (because / although / while / when / if)
- I want to study abroad **because** I want international experience.
- **Although** the job market is competitive, skilled developers can find good jobs.
- **While** some people prefer offices, others like working from home.
- **When** I finished my degree, I started learning web development.

**টিপ:** Speaking-এ প্রতি উত্তরে অন্তত একটা **because** বা **although** sentence রাখো।

---

## 7. Conditionals

| Type | কখন | গঠন | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | সবসময় সত্য | If + present, present | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| **First** | বাস্তব সম্ভাবনা (ভবিষ্যৎ) | If + present, will + V1 | If I get 6.5, I **will apply** to German universities. |
| **Second** | কাল্পনিক / অসম্ভব (বর্তমান) | If + past, would + V1 | If I **were** rich, I **would travel** the world. |
| **Third** | অতীতের কাল্পনিক (যা ঘটেনি) | If + had + V3, would have + V3 | If I **had started** IELTS earlier, I **would have gone** abroad already. |

**মনে রাখো:**
- If clause-এ **will** বসে না: ❌ If I **will get** → ✅ If I **get**
- Second conditional-এ সব subject-এর সাথে **were**: If I **were** you, I would…

---

## 8. Passive Voice

**কখন:** কে কাজটা করেছে সেটা গুরুত্বপূর্ণ না, কাজটাই গুরুত্বপূর্ণ। Writing Task 1 (process, chart) আর formal writing-এ খুব লাগে।

**গঠন:** be (tense অনুযায়ী) + V3

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | They **build** websites. | Websites **are built**. |
| Past simple | They **collected** the data. | The data **was collected**. |
| Present perfect | They **have launched** the app. | The app **has been launched**. |
| Future | They **will release** it. | It **will be released**. |
| Modal | We **can solve** this. | This **can be solved**. |

- The survey **was conducted** in 2020.
- English **is spoken** in many countries.

---

## 9. Linking Words

| কাজ | Linking words |
|---|---|
| যোগ করা | and, also, in addition, moreover, furthermore |
| বিপরীত | but, however, although, on the other hand, whereas |
| কারণ | because, since, as, due to + noun |
| ফলাফল | so, therefore, as a result, consequently |
| উদ্দেশ্য | to, in order to, so that |
| উদাহরণ | for example, for instance, such as |
| উপসংহার | in conclusion, overall, to sum up |

⚠️ **তোমার ভুল: "so" বনাম "so that"**
- **so** = তাই / ফলে (result)
  - I was tired, **so** I went to bed early.
- **so that** = যাতে (purpose), পরে সাধারণত can/could/will/would বসে
  - I am learning English **so that** I **can** study in Europe.
- ❌ It was raining, **so that** I stayed home. → ✅ It was raining, **so** I stayed home.

**Punctuation:**
- **However**, **Therefore**, **Moreover** → sentence-এর শুরুতে, পরে comma।
  - The salary is good. **However,** the workload is heavy.
- **because of / due to** + noun: **Due to** heavy traffic, I was late.
- **because** + full clause: I was late **because** the traffic was heavy.

# ধাপ ৩: Polish

## 10. Comparatives & Superlatives

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| ছোট (১ syllable): fast | fast**er** than | the fast**est** |
| -y শেষ: easy | eas**ier** than | the eas**iest** |
| বড় (২+ syllable): expensive | **more** expensive than | **the most** expensive |
| Irregular: good / bad | better / worse than | the best / the worst |

- ❌ more easier → ✅ easier
- ❌ most best → ✅ the best
- Living in Europe is **more expensive than** living in Bangladesh.
- It was **the best** decision of my life.

**Band বাড়াতে:** much / far / slightly + comparative
- Germany is **much cheaper** than the UK for students.

---

## 11. Gerund (V-ing) vs Infinitive (to + V1)

| Gerund (V-ing) লাগে | Infinitive (to + V1) লাগে |
|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest, consider, keep, practise | want, decide, plan, hope, need, would like, expect, learn |
| I **enjoy coding**. | I **want to study** abroad. |
| I **finished working** at 6 pm. | I **decided to learn** English. |

- **Preposition-এর পরে সবসময় V-ing:**
  - I'm interested **in learning** AI.
  - I'm good **at solving** problems.
  - I'm looking forward **to hearing** from you. (এখানে "to" preposition)
- **Subject হিসেবে V-ing:** **Learning** a new language takes time.

---

## 12. Modal Verbs

**নিয়ম:** Modal-এর পরে সবসময় V1 (to বসে না, s বসে না)।
- ❌ He can **speaks**. / I must **to go**. → ✅ He can **speak**. / I must **go**.

| Modal | অর্থ | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| can / could | পারা, সম্ভাবনা | I **can** build full-stack apps. |
| should | উচিত (পরামর্শ) | The government **should** invest in education. |
| must / have to | বাধ্যতা | Students **have to** show proof of funds. |
| might / may | হয়তো | I **might** move to Germany next year. |
| would | কাল্পনিক, ভদ্রভাবে চাওয়া | I **would like** to work in a multinational company. |

**IELTS টিপ:** মত দেওয়ার সময় সবকিছু ১০০% নিশ্চিতভাবে না বলে might / may / could দিয়ে নরম করো। এতে উত্তর academic শোনায়।
- Technology **may** reduce some jobs, but it **could** also create new ones.

# বোনাস: Capitalization ও Spelling

⚠️ **তোমার ভুল:**
- সবসময় বড় হাতের **I** লিখবে: ❌ i think → ✅ **I** think
- Sentence-এর শুরু, নাম, দেশ, ভাষা, মাস, দিন বড় হাতে: **E**nglish, **G**ermany, **M**onday, **J**une
- Tech নামের সঠিক spelling:

| ✅ সঠিক | ❌ ভুল |
|---|---|
| JavaScript | Javascript, java script |
| PostgreSQL | Postgresql, postgre sql |
| Express.js | ExpressJs, express js |
| Next.js | NextJs, next js |
| React | react js (formal লেখায়) |
| MongoDB | Mongodb, mongo db |
| WordPress | Wordpress |
| PHP | Php |
