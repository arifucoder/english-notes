## 1. Present Tenses

### Present Simple: অভ্যাস, সাধারণ সত্য, রুটিন
**গঠন:** Subject + verb (he/she/it হলে verb + **s/es**)

- I **work** in a bank.
- She **works** from 9 to 5.
- Water **boils** at 100°C.

**Negative/Question:** do / does
- He **doesn't** like coffee. (❌ He doesn't likes)
- **Does** she **work** here?

### Present Continuous: এখন চলছে, সাময়িক
**গঠন:** am / is / are + verb-**ing**

- I **am writing** an email now.
- She **is working** from home this week.

### ⚠️ কখন কোনটা?

| Simple (সবসময়/রুটিন) | Continuous (এখন/সাময়িক) |
|---|---|
| I **drink** tea every morning. | I **am drinking** tea now. |
| He **lives** in Dhaka. | He **is living** with his uncle for a month. |

### ⚠️ State verbs সাধারণত continuous হয় না
know, understand, like, love, want, need, believe, belong, mean

- ✅ I **understand**. ❌ I am understanding.
- ✅ I **know** him. ❌ I am knowing him.

## 2. Past Tenses

### Past Simple: অতীতে শেষ হয়ে গেছে
**গঠন:** verb + **ed** (অথবা irregular: go → went, see → saw)

- I **finished** the report yesterday.
- We **went** to Cox's Bazar last year.

**Negative/Question:** did + **base verb**
- I **didn't go**. (❌ I didn't went)
- **Did** you **call** him?

### Past Continuous: অতীতে একটা নির্দিষ্ট সময়ে চলছিল
**গঠন:** was / were + verb-**ing**

- At 8 pm, I **was having** dinner.
- I **was working** when the power **went** out.

> 💡 লম্বা কাজ → past continuous, মাঝখানে ছোট ঘটনা → past simple

### used to: আগে করতাম, এখন করি না
- I **used to** smoke. (এখন খাই না)
- ⚠️ ❌ I am used to go → এর মানে আলাদা (নিচে দেখুন)

**be/get used to + -ing** = অভ্যস্ত হওয়া
- I'**m used to working** late. (আমি দেরিতে কাজ করতে অভ্যস্ত)

## 3. Present Perfect ⭐ (অফিসে খুব জরুরি)

**গঠন:** have / has + **past participle** (V3)

### কখন ব্যবহার করবেন
**ক) অতীতে হয়েছে, কিন্তু ফলাফল এখনো আছে / সময় বলা নেই**
- I **have sent** the email. (পাঠানো হয়ে গেছে, এখন inbox-এ আছে)
- She **has lost** her phone.

**খ) অভিজ্ঞতা (জীবনে কখনো)**
- **Have** you ever **been** to India?
- I **have** never **used** this software.

**গ) অতীত থেকে এখন পর্যন্ত চলছে** (for / since)
- I **have worked** here **for** three years.
- I **have known** him **since** 2020.

### for নাকি since?
| for = সময়ের পরিমাণ | since = শুরুর সময় |
|---|---|
| for two hours | since 9 am |
| for five years | since 2021 |
| for a long time | since Monday |

### ⚠️ Present Perfect নাকি Past Simple?
নির্দিষ্ট অতীত সময় (yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago) থাকলে **past simple**।

| ✅ | ❌ |
|---|---|
| I **sent** it **yesterday**. | I have sent it yesterday. |
| I **have** already **sent** it. | — |

### already / yet / just
- I've **just** finished. (এইমাত্র)
- I've **already** finished. (আগেই, প্রত্যাশার আগে)
- Have you finished **yet**? / I haven't finished **yet**. (প্রশ্ন ও negative-এ)

### Present Perfect Continuous: কতক্ষণ ধরে চলছে
**গঠন:** have / has been + verb-**ing**
- I **have been waiting** for 30 minutes.
- She'**s been working** on this project since March.

## 4. Past Perfect: অতীতের আগের অতীত

**গঠন:** had + **V3**

- When I reached the station, the train **had** already **left**.
  (আগে ট্রেন ছেড়েছে → তারপর আমি পৌঁছালাম)
- I **had** never **seen** snow before I went to Nepal.

## 5. Future

| গঠন | কখন | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| **will** | তাৎক্ষণিক সিদ্ধান্ত, প্রতিশ্রুতি, অনুমান | I'**ll** call you back. / It **will** rain. |
| **going to** | আগে থেকে ঠিক করা পরিকল্পনা, প্রমাণ দেখে অনুমান | I'**m going to** apply for that job. / Look at the clouds, it'**s going to** rain. |
| **Present continuous** | নির্দিষ্ট arrangement (সময়, জায়গা ঠিক) | I'**m meeting** the client at 3 pm tomorrow. |
| **Present simple** | Timetable, schedule | The flight **leaves** at 6 am. |

### Future Continuous / Future Perfect
- This time tomorrow, I'**ll be flying** to Dubai. (চলমান থাকবে)
- By Friday, I'**ll have finished** the report. (শেষ হয়ে যাবে)

### ⚠️ when / if / after / before এর পরে will বসে না
- ✅ I'll call you **when** I **arrive**.
- ❌ I'll call you when I will arrive.

## 6. Modal Verbs

Modal-এর পরে সবসময় **base verb** (to নয়, s নয়)।
- ✅ She **can speak** English. ❌ She can speaks / can to speak

| Modal | মানে | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| **can** | পারা, অনুমতি | I **can** drive. / **Can** I come in? |
| **could** | অতীতে পারতাম, ভদ্র অনুরোধ, সম্ভাবনা | **Could** you help me? / It **could** be true. |
| **may / might** | হয়তো (সম্ভাবনা) | I **might** be late. |
| **must** | অবশ্যই (নিজের মনে হওয়া), নিশ্চিত অনুমান | I **must** finish this today. / He **must** be tired. |
| **have to** | বাইরের নিয়মে বাধ্য | I **have to** wear a uniform. |
| **don't have to** | দরকার নেই | You **don't have to** come. |
| **mustn't** | করা নিষেধ | You **mustn't** smoke here. |
| **should** | উচিত (পরামর্শ) | You **should** see a doctor. |
| **would** | ভদ্র অনুরোধ, কাল্পনিক | **Would** you like some tea? |

### ⚠️ don't have to ≠ mustn't
- You **don't have to** come. = না এলেও চলবে
- You **mustn't** come. = আসা নিষেধ

### অতীত নিয়ে অনুমান: modal + have + V3
- He **must have forgotten**. (নিশ্চয়ই ভুলে গেছে)
- You **should have told** me. (বলা উচিত ছিল, বলোনি)
- She **might have missed** the bus.

## 7. If (Conditionals)

| ধরন | গঠন | মানে | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | If + present, present | সবসময় সত্য | If you heat ice, it **melts**. |
| **First** | If + present, **will** + verb | বাস্তব সম্ভাবনা | If it **rains**, I'**ll stay** home. |
| **Second** | If + past, **would** + verb | কাল্পনিক (বর্তমান) | If I **had** money, I **would buy** a car. |
| **Third** | If + had + V3, **would have** + V3 | অতীতের আফসোস | If I **had studied**, I **would have passed**. |

- ⚠️ If I **were** you, I would accept the offer. (I/he/she এর সাথেও **were**)
- **unless** = if not → I won't go **unless** you come.

### wish
- I **wish** I **had** more time. (এখন নেই)
- I **wish** I **had studied** harder. (অতীতের আফসোস)

## 8. Passive Voice

**গঠন:** be + **V3**। কে করেছে তার চেয়ে কী হয়েছে বেশি জরুরি হলে।

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | They **clean** the office daily. | The office **is cleaned** daily. |
| Past simple | Someone **stole** my bag. | My bag **was stolen**. |
| Present perfect | They **have approved** the plan. | The plan **has been approved**. |
| Future | We **will send** the invoice. | The invoice **will be sent**. |
| Modal | You **must sign** the form. | The form **must be signed**. |
| Continuous | They **are repairing** the road. | The road **is being repaired**. |

> 💡 Office email ও report-এ passive খুব কাজে লাগে: *"The payment **has been received**."*

## 9. Reported Speech (অন্যের কথা বলা)

সাধারণত tense এক ধাপ পিছিয়ে যায়।

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "I **am** busy." | He said (that) he **was** busy. |
| "I **will** call you." | She said she **would** call me. |
| "I **have** finished." | He said he **had** finished. |
| "I **can** help." | She said she **could** help. |

### say নাকি tell?
- He **said** (that)... (কাউকে উল্লেখ ছাড়া)
- He **told me** (that)... (tell-এর পরে মানুষ লাগে)
- ❌ He said me / ❌ He told that

### প্রশ্ন ও অনুরোধ
- "Where do you live?" → She asked **where I lived**. (প্রশ্নের word order না)
- "Are you free?" → He asked **if** I **was** free.
- "Please wait." → He asked me **to wait**.

## 10. Questions

### সাধারণ প্রশ্ন
**Question word + auxiliary + subject + verb**
- **Where do you** work?
- **What did she** say?
- **Why are you** late?

### ⚠️ Subject নিয়ে প্রশ্ন করলে do/does/did লাগে না
- **Who called** you? (কে ফোন করেছে, who = subject)
- **Who did** you **call**? (তুমি কাকে ফোন করেছ)

### Indirect Questions (ভদ্র প্রশ্ন) ⭐
প্রশ্নের ভেতরে প্রশ্ন হলে **সাধারণ sentence-এর order**।
- Where is the meeting room? → Could you tell me **where the meeting room is**?
- ❌ Could you tell me where is the meeting room?
- Do you know **what time it starts**?

### Tag Questions
- You're coming, **aren't you**?
- She didn't call, **did she**?

## 11. -ing নাকি to + verb

| এদের পরে **-ing** | এদের পরে **to + verb** |
|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest, consider, keep, practise, deny, miss | want, decide, plan, hope, agree, promise, refuse, need, expect, manage |
| I **enjoy reading**. | I **want to learn**. |
| Would you **mind waiting**? | We **decided to hire** him. |

### Preposition-এর পরে সবসময় -ing
- I'm interested **in learning** English.
- Thank you **for helping** me.
- I look forward **to hearing** from you. ⚠️ (❌ to hear)

### মানে বদলে যায়
- I **stopped smoking**. (ধূমপান ছেড়ে দিলাম)
- I **stopped to smoke**. (ধূমপান করতে থামলাম)
- **Remember to lock** the door. (ভবিষ্যতে করতে মনে রেখো)
- I **remember locking** it. (করেছিলাম, মনে আছে)

### make / let + base verb
- My boss **made me work** late. (❌ made me to work)
- **Let me know**.

## 12. Articles (a / an / the) ⚠️⚠️

### a / an: অনির্দিষ্ট, প্রথমবার, singular countable
- **a** + consonant **sound**: a book, a **u**niversity (ইউ sound)
- **an** + vowel **sound**: an apple, an **h**our (h silent), an MBA

### the: নির্দিষ্ট, দুজনেই জানে কোনটা
- I bought **a** laptop. **The** laptop is very fast. (দ্বিতীয়বার)
- Please close **the** door. (এই ঘরের দরজা)
- **The** sun, **the** moon, **the** world (একটাই আছে)
- **The** best, **the** first, **the** same

### কোনো article নেই (zero article)
- সাধারণভাবে বললে plural / uncountable: I like **coffee**. **Dogs** are friendly.
- ভাষা, খাবার, খেলা: I speak **English**. Let's have **lunch**. I play **cricket**.
- go to **work / school / bed / hospital** (উদ্দেশ্য অনুযায়ী)

### জায়গার নামে
| the লাগে | the লাগে না |
|---|---|
| **the** USA, **the** UK, **the** Netherlands | Bangladesh, India, Japan |
| **the** Padma, **the** Bay of Bengal | Dhaka, Asia |
| **the** Himalayas | Mount Everest |

### ⚠️ কমন ভুল
- ❌ I am engineer. → ✅ I am **an** engineer.
- ❌ She is best employee. → ✅ She is **the** best employee.

## 13. Countable / Uncountable ⚠️

| Countable (গোনা যায়) | Uncountable (গোনা যায় না) |
|---|---|
| a job, two jobs | work |
| a suggestion, suggestions | advice |
| a fact, facts | information |
| a word, words | vocabulary |
| a suitcase, bags | luggage |
| a chair, chairs | furniture |
| a dollar, dollars | money |

### ⚠️ Uncountable-এ
- **s** বসে না: ❌ informations, advices, vocabularies, furnitures
- **a/an** বসে না: ❌ an advice → ✅ **a piece of** advice / some advice
- verb singular: The information **is** correct.

## 14. Quantifiers

| Countable | Uncountable | দুটোতেই |
|---|---|---|
| **many** people | **much** time | **a lot of / lots of** |
| **a few** friends (কিছু আছে) | **a little** money (কিছু আছে) | **some** (positive) |
| **few** friends (প্রায় নেই) | **little** money (প্রায় নেই) | **any** (negative/question) |
| **number of** | **amount of** | **enough** |

- I have **some** questions. / Do you have **any** questions? / I don't have **any** questions.
- ⚠️ অনুরোধ বা অফারে প্রশ্ন হলেও **some**: Would you like **some** tea?

### all / both / each / every / either / neither
- **Every** employee **has** an ID. (every + singular)
- **Both** options are good. / **Neither** option is good. / **Either** day is fine.

## 15. Pronouns

| Subject | Object | Possessive adj. | Possessive | Reflexive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

- ⚠️ **its** (তার) ≠ **it's** (it is)
- ⚠️ **their** (তাদের) ≠ **there** (সেখানে) ≠ **they're** (they are)
- a friend **of mine** (আমার এক বন্ধু)

## 16. Relative Clauses (who, which, that, whose, where)

| শব্দ | কার জন্য | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | মানুষ | The man **who** called you is my boss. |
| **which** | জিনিস | The laptop **which** I bought is fast. |
| **that** | মানুষ বা জিনিস | The report **that** you sent was great. |
| **whose** | যার (মালিকানা) | She's the colleague **whose** idea won. |
| **where** | জায়গা | This is the office **where** I work. |

- Object হলে who/which/that বাদ দেওয়া যায়: The report (that) you sent...
- Comma-সহ extra তথ্যে **that** বসে না: My manager, **who** is from Sylhet, is very kind.

## 17. Adjectives ও Adverbs

- **Adjective** → noun-কে বর্ণনা করে: She is a **careful** driver.
- **Adverb** → verb-কে বর্ণনা করে: She drives **carefully**.

| Adjective | Adverb |
|---|---|
| quick | quickly |
| good | **well** ⚠️ |
| fast | fast |
| hard | hard (hardly = প্রায় না!) |

- ⚠️ ❌ She speaks English very good. → ✅ very **well**

### -ed নাকি -ing?
- I am **bored**. (আমার অনুভূতি)
- The meeting is **boring**. (যেটা অনুভূতি তৈরি করে)
- ⚠️ ❌ I am boring → মানে "আমি বিরক্তিকর মানুষ"!

### Adjective-এর ক্রম
**Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Colour → Origin → Material → Noun**
- a **nice big old** wooden table

## 18. Comparison

| ধরন | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| ছোট শব্দ | cheap**er** than | **the** cheap**est** |
| লম্বা শব্দ | **more** expensive than | **the most** expensive |
| Irregular | better, worse, further | the best, the worst, the furthest |

- ⚠️ ❌ more better → ✅ better
- **as ... as**: This phone is **as good as** that one. / not **as** expensive **as**
- **The more** you practise, **the better** you get.

---

## 19. Word Order ⚠️

### মূল নিয়ম: Subject + Verb + Object + Place + Time
- I **met** my manager **at the office yesterday**.
- ❌ I yesterday met... (বাংলার মতো সময় মাঝখানে বসে না)

### Verb আর object আলাদা হয় না
- ✅ I speak English **very well**. ❌ I speak very well English.

### Frequency adverbs (always, usually, often, never)
- main verb-এর **আগে**: I **always** check my email.
- be verb-এর **পরে**: She is **always** on time.

### still / yet / already / even / also
- I **also** work on weekends. / She is **also** a designer.

## 20. Conjunctions ও Linking Words

| কাজ | শব্দ | উদাহরণ |
|---|---|---|
| বৈপরীত্য | although, though, even though, however, but | **Although** it was late, we continued. |
| বৈপরীত্য (+ noun/-ing) | despite, in spite of | **Despite** the rain, we went out. |
| কারণ | because, since, as, due to (+ noun) | The meeting was cancelled **due to** the strike. |
| ফলাফল | so, therefore, as a result | **Therefore**, we need more time. |
| উদ্দেশ্য | to, in order to, so that | I came early **so that** I could prepare. |
| যোগ | and, also, in addition, moreover | **In addition**, the cost is low. |

- ⚠️ ❌ Although it was late, but we continued. → although আর but একসাথে নয়।
- ⚠️ ❌ Despite of → ✅ despite / in spite of

## 21. Prepositions ⚠️

### সময়: at / on / in
| at (নির্দিষ্ট মুহূর্ত) | on (দিন, তারিখ) | in (লম্বা সময়) |
|---|---|---|
| at 5 pm | on Monday | in October |
| at night | on 15 March | in 2026 |
| at the weekend (UK) | on my birthday | in the morning |
| at lunchtime | on Friday evening | in summer |

- next / last / this / every এর আগে কিছু বসে না: ❌ on next Monday → ✅ **next Monday**

### জায়গা: at / on / in
| at (বিন্দু) | on (পৃষ্ঠ) | in (ভেতরে) |
|---|---|---|
| at the door | on the table | in the room |
| at the bus stop | on the wall | in Dhaka |
| at work, at home | on the second floor | in a car |
| at the meeting | on a bus / train / plane | in the newspaper |

### অন্যান্য সময়ের preposition
- **by** Friday (শুক্রবারের মধ্যে, deadline) vs **until** Friday (শুক্রবার পর্যন্ত চলবে)
- **during** the meeting (সময়ের মধ্যে) vs **for** two hours (কতক্ষণ)
- **within** a week (এক সপ্তাহের মধ্যে)

### Adjective + preposition (মুখস্থ রাখুন)
| | | |
|---|---|---|
| good **at** | interested **in** | afraid **of** |
| responsible **for** | familiar **with** | aware **of** |
| married **to** | different **from** | angry **with** (someone) |

### Verb + preposition
| | | |
|---|---|---|
| depend **on** | apply **for** (a job) | listen **to** |
| wait **for** | agree **with** (someone) | belong **to** |
| apologize **for** | look **at** | deal **with** |

### ⚠️ যেখানে preposition লাগে না
- ❌ discuss **about** → ✅ discuss the plan
- ❌ contact **to** → ✅ contact me
- ❌ enter **into** the room → ✅ enter the room
- ❌ reach **to** office → ✅ reach the office
