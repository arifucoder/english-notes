# Auxiliary Verb ও WH-Question

---

## ১. Auxiliary Verb কী?

**Auxiliary Verb** মানে সাহায্যকারী ক্রিয়া (**Helping Verb**)।

এগুলো Main Verb-কে **Tense**, **Question** আর **Negative** বানাতে সাহায্য করে।

Auxiliary Verb প্রধানত ২ ধরনের: **Primary** আর **Modal**। এর সাথে **WH-word** কীভাবে কাজ করে, সেটাও নিচে আছে।

---

### A. Primary Auxiliary Verbs

এই তিনটা মূল Verb:

| Verb | Forms |
|---|---|
| **Be** | am, is, are, was, were, be, been, being |
| **Do** | do, does, did |
| **Have** | have, has, had |

---

### B. Modal Auxiliary Verbs

এগুলোও Helping Verb হিসেবে কাজ করে।

| Modal | Example |
|---|---|
| **Can** | Can you swim? |
| **Could** | Could you help me? |
| **Will** | Will you come? |
| **Would** | Would you like coffee? |
| **Should** | Should I go? |
| **Must** | You must work. |
| **May** | May I come in? |
| **Might** | It might rain. |
| **Shall** | Shall we start? |

---

### C. WH-Words

⚠️ এগুলো **Auxiliary Verb না**। এগুলো দিয়ে প্রশ্ন করা হয়।

| WH-word | অর্থ |
|---|---|
| **What** | কী |
| **When** | কখন |
| **Where** | কোথায় |
| **Why** | কেন |
| **Who** | কে |
| **Whom** | কাকে |
| **Whose** | কার |
| **Which** | কোনটি |
| **How** | কীভাবে / কেমন |

---

## ২. WH-word-এর পরে কি সবসময় Auxiliary Verb বসে?

**না।** সাধারণত দুইটা গুরুত্বপূর্ণ Structure আছে।

---

### Structure 1: WH + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb

| WH | Auxiliary | Subject | Main Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | do | you | do? |
| Where | do | you | live? |
| When | did | you | start working? |
| Why | are | you | learning English? |
| How | have | you | improved your skills? |

**Examples:**
- What **do** you do?
- Where **do** you live?
- When **did** you start working?
- Why **are** you learning English?
- How **have** you improved your skills?

---

### Structure 2: WH + Main Verb

যখন WH-word **নিজেই Subject** হিসেবে কাজ করে, তখন সাধারণত আলাদা Auxiliary লাগে না।

| WH (Subject) | Main Verb | বাকি অংশ |
|---|---|---|
| Who | called | you? |
| Who | built | this website? |
| What | happened | yesterday? |
| Which developer | created | this application? |

**Examples:**
- Who **called** you?
- Who **built** this website?
- What **happened** yesterday?
- Which developer **created** this application?

এখানে **Who** বা **What** নিজেই Subject।
